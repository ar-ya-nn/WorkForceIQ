WorkForceIQ logo options
------------------------
The site uses frontend/public/logo.svg (browser tab, sidebar, login page, loading screen).

To switch logo: copy one of these files into frontend/public/, delete the old logo.svg,
and rename your copy to logo.svg. Nothing else needs changing.

  logo-a-rising-w.svg       A - Rising W : a W whose last stroke rises into an "i" for insight
  logo-b-constellation.svg  B - Team constellation: three linked people, one rising and lit
  logo-c-lens.svg           C - Insight lens (current): W monogram in a glass lens with an AI spark
  logo-d-pulse.svg          D - Workforce pulse: a heartbeat line shaped like a W
